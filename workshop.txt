title=Animal Tracks Outline
description=Highlights animals with an outline when Search Mode is on and the focus is set to Animal Tracks.
description=
description=Outline transparency depends on the Tracking skill (0-10): no outline at level 0; base 10% at level 1, ramps up to about 43% at level 10.
description=
description=Tracking animals is a difficult skill, which is further limited by the game's visuals. Personally, I can't see animals at all behind the textures of the forest. This mod makes it easier to find animals with your eyes. In addition, it's not a cheat, as the brightness of the outline depends on the corresponding skill.
description=
description=v0.1 - single player only.
description=v0.2 - The radius for drawing animal outlines has been increased to 50.
description=v0.3 - Animal carcasses are also traced using the same logic.
description=
description=v0.4 - Updated for game build 42.20.0. The mod had stopped working completely.
description=
description=IsoCell.getObjectList() now returns a Set, which Lua cannot index. The loop that finds animals threw an error on every frame and took the rest of the update with it, so neither animals nor carcasses were ever outlined. It uses getObjectListForLua() now.
description=
description=Carcasses are no longer rescanned every single frame. That sweep walks over ten thousand squares, and it now runs on an interval instead - carcasses do not move, so nothing is lost and a lot of frames are gained.
description=
description=Living animals now fade out over a second when they leave your field of view, instead of vanishing in one abrupt step. Carcasses are unchanged: they stay lit whether you are looking at them or not, which is the whole point of spotting one through the tree crowns.
description=
description=Lua errors are no longer swallowed; they go to console.txt so the next breakage is visible.
description=
