title=Animal Tracks Outline
description=Highlights animals with an outline when Search Mode is on and the focus is set to Animal Tracks.
description=
description=Outline transparency depends on the Tracking skill (0-10): no outline at level 0; base 10% at level 1, ramps up to about 43% at level 10.
description=
description=Tracking animals is a difficult skill, which is further limited by the game's visuals. Personally, I can't see animals at all behind the textures of the forest. This mod makes it easier to find animals with your eyes. In addition, it's not a cheat, as the brightness of the outline depends on the corresponding skill.

In split-screen every player gets outlines based on their own Search Mode and their own Tracking skill, on their part of the screen.
